Storage and cookies

Last updated 2026-09-19

The game stores data on your device so it can remember your work and your settings — this is where all of your content lives.

  • App storage: your content, preferences (theme, text size, motion, captions) and, if you bought one, your purchase entitlement.
  • This website: serves static pages only. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party scripts.

Clearing the game's data or uninstalling removes everything stored locally.
