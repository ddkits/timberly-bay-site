Children's privacy

Last updated 2026-09-19

Timberly Bay is rated 7+ (Everyone 10+ on Google Play, 9+ on the App Store)(9+ on the App Store)(Everyone 10+ on Google Play).

This game is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live), and in any case nothing leaves the device. There is no chat, no user-to-user messaging, no social login and no behavioural advertising. Purchases sit behind the device's own parental gate and store authentication.
