Timberly Bay

A big 3D adventure for little heroes

Timberly Bay — a bright 3D platform adventure for kids 7+. Six animal heroes each have a power and two levels of their own in every world: Milo runs along walls and glides, Nell swims against the current and dives, Buzz hovers up floating flowers, Rex climbs cliffs and pounds crates, Pip jumps sky-high and dashes, Wren double-jumps and glides far. Three worlds — a sunny harbour, treetops above the clouds and a glowing crystal reef — 39 levels with stars, gems and crowns to win, and a monster to bonk at the end of each world. Explore the whole harbour town too: missions, friends to meet and a clubhouse that grows. No way to lose: a fall puts you back at the last flag. Plays offline, in English, Arabic, French and Persian.
